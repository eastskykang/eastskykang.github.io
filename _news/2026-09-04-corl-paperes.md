Excited to announce that two collaborative papers with [Taerim Yoon](https://taerimyoon.me/) have been accepted to CoRL 2026: ["Teaching Robots Like Dogs: Learning Agile Navigation from Luring, Gesture, and Speech"](https://taerimyoon.me/Teaching-Robots-Like-Dogs-Learning-Agile-Navigation-from-Luring-Gesture-and-Speech/) and ["Learning Unions of Convex Sets via Invertible Latent Decomposition for Path Planning."](https://taerimyoon.me/Learning-Unions-of-Convex-Sets-via-Invertible-Latent-Decomposition-for-Path-Planning/) Thank you to all the co-authors, and huge congratulations to Taerim for leading these amazing projects!
