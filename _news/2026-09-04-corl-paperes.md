Excited to announce that two collaborative papers with [Taerim Yoon](https://taerimyoon.me/) have been accepted to CoRL 2026: ["Teaching Robots Like Dogs,"](https://taerimyoon.me/Teaching-Robots-Like-Dogs-Learning-Agile-Navigation-from-Luring-Gesture-and-Speech/) and "Learning Unions of Convex Sets via Invertible Latent Decomposition for Path Planning." Huge congratulations to Taerim for leading both projects and to all the co-authors for their fantastic work!
